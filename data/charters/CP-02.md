# CP-02 Sales Copilot

## Objective
Help account managers prepare for client calls faster using CRM notes and product sheets.

## Scope
Call preparation summaries and product comparisons. Pricing approvals are out of scope.

## Success metrics
Adoption above 50% of licensed sellers. Shorter call preparation time.

## Risks
Low trust from senior sellers. CRM notes are incomplete, so summaries can be thin.

## Sponsor
Head of Sales Operations
