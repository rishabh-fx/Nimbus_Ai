# BOT-01 IT Helpdesk Bot

## Objective
Resolve common IT requests such as password resets and VPN issues without an engineer.

## Scope
Level 1 IT requests. Hardware replacement is out of scope.

## Success metrics
Deflect level 1 tickets. Weekly active users above 60% of licensed seats.

## Risks
Integration with the ticketing tool may break after upgrades.

## Sponsor
Chief Information Officer
