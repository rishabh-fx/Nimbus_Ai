# CP-01 HR Policy Copilot

## Objective
Let employees get accurate answers to HR policy questions without raising a ticket.

## Scope
Leave, travel and benefits policies for India employees. Payroll disputes are out of scope.

## Success metrics
Weekly active users above 60% of licensed seats. Fewer HR tickets on policy questions.

## Risks
Policy documents going out of date. Employees trusting an answer without checking the source.

## Sponsor
Chief People Officer
