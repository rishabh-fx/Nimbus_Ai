# PM-01 Demand Forecasting Model

## Objective
Improve weekly demand forecasts so inventory planners order the right stock.

## Scope
Top 500 SKUs across three regions. Promotions planning is out of scope.

## Success metrics
Lower forecast error than the current spreadsheet method. Fewer stock-outs.

## Risks
Data pipeline costs higher than planned. Planners may keep overriding the forecast.
