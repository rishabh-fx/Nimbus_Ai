# PM-02 Churn Predictor

## Objective
Flag customers likely to cancel so the retention team can act early.

## Scope
Subscription customers in India. Pricing changes are out of scope.

## Success metrics
Retention team contacts flagged customers within a week. Lower churn in the flagged group.

## Risks
Model is new, so early results may not hold. Retention team capacity is limited.

## Sponsor
Head of Customer Retention
